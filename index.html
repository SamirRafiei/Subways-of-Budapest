Subways of Budapest

Game Rules

The goal is to build 4 metro lines (M1–M4) and collect as many points as possible.

  • On the menu, enter your name and press Start Game.
  • On the game screen, each round belongs to one metro line.
  • Press Draw Next Card to reveal a station card.
  • After drawing a card, click a valid station on the board to draw one segment.
  • The first segment of a round must start from the highlighted starting station of the current line.
  • Later segments must start from an endpoint of the current line.
  • Segments must be straight (horizontal/vertical) or diagonal at 45°, and may not pass through other stations.
  • Stations must match the card letter, except Joker cards and the Joker station (Deák tér).
  • Segments cannot cross or be parallel between the same two stations, and a line cannot re-enter its own stations.
  • After 8 cards in a round, you must press Next Round to continue with the next metro line.
  • After all 4 rounds, scores are calculated and saved; they can be viewed from the menu.
